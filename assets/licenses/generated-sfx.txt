Original Neon Relic sound effects: plasma-shot.wav, enemy-shatter.wav,
orbital-contact.wav, blade-swish.wav, prism-beam.wav, gravity-open.wav,
gravity-loop.wav, prism-loop.wav, energy-impact.wav, player-hurt.wav,
enemy-windup.wav and crystal-pickup.wav.
Generated by this project's tools/build_audio.py using deterministic procedural
oscillators and seeded noise. These twelve audio assets are provided under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The script also converts Kenney Digital Audio originals from OGG to mono 16-bit
PCM WAV, preserving sample rate, duration and amplitude. Kenney originals and
converted samples retain the original CC0 license; see kenney-digital-audio.txt.
The thunder sample has a separate CC BY 3.0 license; see jerimee-thunder.txt.
